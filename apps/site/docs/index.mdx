# Welcome to the Hafifa 👋

This is your guide through training in the fullstack department.

## How it works

Throughout the training you build **one project: an Instagram-style app**. Every stage teaches a new
part of the stack and ends with a **milestone** that adds that part to your app.

1. **Learn**: read, watch and try the materials for the stage.
2. **Practice**: solve the small exercises.
3. **Build**: complete the stage's Instagram milestone in your project in the training repo.
4. **Review**: open a merge request and assign your trainer. Once it is approved, move on to the
   next stage.

Your **trainer** is a developer from your team. They review your code and answer your questions,
so don't stay stuck for long. Ask!

## Choose your track

Some stages differ between teams (for example React or Angular). Choose your track in your profile menu (your name, top right).
The sidebar and the pages then show only what is relevant to you. If you're not sure, ask your
trainer.
