<Milestone title="Instagram in Angular">

Rebuild the Instagram client as an Angular app that talks to your API from the NestJS stage. Reuse
the SCSS from the HTML & CSS stage.

- **Feed** (`/`): the posts of the people you follow, with likes and comments.
- **Profile** (`/users/:username`): the user's details and a grid of their posts.
- **Post** (`/posts/:id`): one post with all its comments.
- **Upload** (`/upload`): a form to publish a new post.

<Checklist
  title="Requirements"
  items={[

    'Routing with route parameters, and a "not found" page for unknown URLs',
    'Components communicate through inputs and outputs; API calls are in services',
