# Stage structure

Every stage follows the same structure, so trainees always know where they are. All the stage
pages already exist with this structure. Fill them in, don't restructure them.

1. **Overview**: `<StageOverview>` with duration, goals and the content owner (you).
2. **Learn**: the materials, as a mix of reading, videos and interactive material. Add a time
   estimate to each one, and mark nice-to-haves as optional.
3. **Practice**: a few small `<Exercise>` blocks that are quick to check.
4. **Milestone**: exactly one `<Milestone>`. This is what the trainee adds to their Instagram
   project, and what the trainer reviews.

## Template

```mdx
---
title: Git & GitLab
---

<StageOverview
  duration="1 week"
  owner="Your name"
  goals={['Work with branches, commits and merges', 'Open and review merge requests in GitLab']}
/>

## Learn

<Resources>
  <Resource type="docs" title="Pro Git, chapters 1-3" href="https://..." time="1.5 h">
    Focus on branching. Skim the history part.
  </Resource>
</Resources>

<Video src="git/branching.mp4" title="Branching explained" duration="12 min" />

## Practice

<Exercise title="Resolve a merge conflict" difficulty="medium">
  Create two branches that change the same line, then merge them.
  <Hint>Look at what `git status` tells you.</Hint>
</Exercise>

<Milestone title="Set up your project">

- Create your project folder in the training repo, on your own branch.
- Open your first merge request and assign your trainer.

</Milestone>
```

## Page kinds

Big stages split their material into pages, grouped into sections (one folder per section, see
`stages/02-html-css`). Give every page a kind in its front matter. The sidebar shows it as an icon:

<PageKindLegend />

```mdx
---
title: 'Quiz: CSS'
sidebar_custom_props: { kind: quiz }
---
```

The kinds are `reading`, `video`, `game`, `practice`, `quiz` and `milestone`. They are defined in
`src/pageKinds/pageKinds.ts`. To add one, add it there and give it an icon in `pageKinds.css`.

A section usually starts with a video or reading, has games and practice in between, and ends with
a short [quiz](./components#quiz). The stage ends with a milestone page.

## Tips for good content

- **Prefer short and focused material over long and complete.** Link to the docs for the details.
- **Explain why, not just how.** Trainees remember a concept when they know what problem it solves.
- **Make the milestone specific.** Write clear acceptance criteria, so the trainee and the trainer
  agree on what "done" means.
- **Links must work inside the closed network.** Use our internal mirrors of the documentation.
